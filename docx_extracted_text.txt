Project Management Consultancy (PMC): Short overview of end-to-end management from planning to handover.

       END-TO-END PROJECT MANAGEMENT CONSULTANCY

      From Planning to Handover - We Manage Everything.

   At CS ASSOCIATES, we are your single-point partner who takes complete ownership of your project. You share your vision; we deliver the building - On Time, Within Budget, With Assured Quality.

OUR 7-STAGE PROCESS:

STAGE 1: PROJECT PLANNING &amp; PRE-CONSTRUCTION

Feasibility study, budget estimation, drawing review, and detailed project schedule. We identify risks before they become costs.

STAGE 2: DESIGN &amp; VENDOR FINALIZATION

Coordinating Architect, Structural, and MEP consultants. Assisting you in finalizing the right contractors and vendors through technical comparison - not just lowest price.

STAGE 3: SITE EXECUTION &amp; SUPERVISION

Daily site supervision by our engineers. We control quality, workmanship, and safety. Daily Work Progress Report (DPR) with photos directly on your WhatsApp.

STAGE 4: QUALITY CONTROL &amp; QUALITY ASSURANCE

Multi-level quality checks for civil, waterproofing, electrical, plumbing, and finishing works. No compromise, no hidden works.

STAGE 5: MATERIAL &amp; BILLING MANAGEMENT

We verify all materials at site, control wastage, and check contractor bills with measurements. Our clients save 8-15% on total project cost.

STAGE 6: MEP &amp; FINISHING COORDINATION

Seamless coordination of all services - Electrical, Plumbing, HVAC, Fire, Automation, Interiors - to ensure perfect finishing and zero rework.

STAGE 7: SNAG LIST &amp; HANDOVER

Final finishing inspection, snag list closure, as-built drawings, warranty documents, and a peaceful, snag-free handover of your keys.

            INDIVIDUAL HOMES &amp; LUXURY VILLAS*

            Crafting Bespoke Homes for Discerning Families_

At *CS ASSOCIATES*, we understand a luxury villa is not just construction - it's your family's legacy. We provide dedicated Project Management for individual homes where every detail matters.

*SERVICES FOR INDIVIDUAL &amp; LUXURY HOMES:*

*1. Personalized Project Planning*

We start with your lifestyle. Detailed planning of space, Vastu, natural light, ventilation, and future needs. Complete budget breakdown and timeline before first brick.

*2. Design Realization*

We work with your Architect to ensure what is drawn is exactly executed on site. No deviation in dimensions, heights, or finishes.

*3. Premium Material Assistance*

Expert guidance in selecting Marble, Granite, Wood Veneer, Designer Tiles, Sanitary Fittings, Lights &amp; Switches. We ensure you get genuine branded materials at best price.

*4. Dedicated Site Supervision*

Your home gets a dedicated engineer. Daily supervision of all works - Structure, Block work, Plastering, Waterproofing, Flooring, Painting, Fabrication &amp; Woodwork.

*5. Luxury Finishing Management*

This is where we specialize. Groove detailing, mitre joints, shadow gaps, profile lighting, seamless flooring, zero-crack finish - We achieve designer-level finishing.

*6. Smart Home &amp; MEP Integration*

Perfect coordination of Home Automation, Security, CCTV, Air Conditioning, Solar, Electrical &amp; Plumbing for a modern, hassle-free luxury life.

*7. Transparent Billing &amp; Cost Saving*

Every bag of cement, every tile, every payment is recorded. We cross-check contractor bills with actual site measurement. Clients save 10-15% overall.

*8. Final Styling &amp; Handover*

Complete snag list checking, deep cleaning, final touch-ups, and peaceful handover with all warranties and drawings.
